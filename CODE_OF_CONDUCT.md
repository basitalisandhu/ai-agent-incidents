# Code of conduct

This project records security failures of real products made by real people and
organisations. That makes a plain, factual tone a requirement, not a preference.

## Expectations

- Be factual. Every claim in a record must be supported by a cited public
  source. Do not speculate about motives, competence or blame.
- Be respectful. Name products, vendors and researchers as the sources do.
  Do not target individuals, and do not add personal data that the sources do
  not already contain.
- Be constructive in review. Disagreements about coding are expected; argue from
  the codebook and the source, not from the person.
- Keep the scope. This is a dataset of public events, not a place to disclose
  new vulnerabilities (see SECURITY.md) or to promote products.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, publishing others'
private information, and sustained disruption of discussions.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide or remove contributions and comments that break
these rules, and may block repeat offenders. Report concerns to the maintainer
through the contact on the GitHub profile of @basitalisandhu. Reports are
handled in confidence.
